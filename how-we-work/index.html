How we work, three of five

From first conversation to final reportFrom first conversation to final report

The steps below show how work normally proceeds with clients. Depending on what’s needed, some projects may only require one or two of these steps.

  1. 01

    Discuss

    An initial conversation to understand what you need, agree the scope of work and provide a quote with an outline of the proposed report.

  2. 02

    Documents

    Your current documents can be reviewed before the site visit to make the most of the time on site.

  3. 03

    Site visit

    A review of every area where batteries are built, tested, stored or moved, observing your team at work and discussing findings as the visit progresses. Where possible, this also includes observing emergency drills and reviewing recent incidents.

  4. 04

    Report

    Every finding sets out what was observed, why it matters and what is recommended, with a priority and timescale for each action.

  5. 05

    Follow up

    Questions about the report can be answered, with remote support available for your team and follow-up visits to review progress if required.
